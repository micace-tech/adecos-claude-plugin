# Google Ads numbers (checked 2026-10)

[Google] = Google Help Center. [Industry] = common practice, not a Google rule. Re-check when Google updates its pages.

## Bidding & learning
- Learning period: current English help page says it takes "a few conversion cycles (1–2 typically)"; other versions say ~50 conversion events or 3 conversion cycles. Treat as unresolved — say "about 1–3 conversion cycles". [Google] support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13020501
- Target CPA can start without conversion history; evaluate it over 30 days with ≥30 conversions. [Google] answer/6268632
- Target ROAS (Search) needs ≥15 conversions in the past 30 days. [Google] answer/6268637
- Smart Bidding evaluation: wait ≥2 weeks without changes; up to 4 weeks with <30 conversions (stated on a Display page, not Search-specific). [Google] answer/10285843
- From 17 Aug 2026: budget-limited target-based campaigns perform more consistently toward the bid target, including when budgets change; Google won't adjust targets or budgets automatically. [Google] answer/17061251
- Maximize Clicks is useful for new campaigns lacking conversion data; Manual CPC suits known profitable keywords. [Google] answer/2472725, answer/6268626
- Change size: keep each budget/target change around ≤20%. [Industry]

## Budget
- A day can spend up to 2× the average daily budget; monthly charge capped at 30.4× daily budget. [Google] answer/2375423

## Ads & quality
- RSA: up to 15 headlines (30 chars) and 4 descriptions (90 chars); minimum 3 + 2; ≥2 RSAs per ad group recommended; pinning not recommended for most advertisers, pin 2–3 assets per position if needed. [Google] answer/7684791
- Quality Score 1–10 (expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page experience, vs last 90 days) is a diagnostic, not an auction input. [Google] answer/6167118

## Keywords
- Broad match should be paired with Smart Bidding. [Google] answer/7478529
- Negative keywords don't match close variants. [Google] answer/2453972
- Pause a non-converting keyword after spending 2–3× target CPA (4–5× for long sales cycles) with ≥~30 clicks. [Industry]
- Review the search terms report weekly while new or high-spend, then every 2 weeks to monthly. [Industry]
